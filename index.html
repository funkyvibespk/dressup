DressUp customer care

Shipping & returns.

Delivery across Pakistan

We prepare each order with care. Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 working days after confirmation; remote areas may take longer. Our team may call to confirm your COD order before dispatch.

Cash on delivery

Pay the final order total in cash to the courier when your parcel arrives. Orders with a merchandise subtotal above PKR 15,000 receive a 5% discount, shown at checkout and deducted from your total.

Size exchanges

Request an exchange within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags and packaging. One size exchange is complimentary where available; otherwise we’ll help you choose an alternative.

Returns and help

If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours with your order number and clear photos. We’ll review it and arrange a suitable exchange or resolution. Customised or worn items cannot be exchanged.
